# Workflow 02 — Delta Audience Research

## Purpose

Research Delta Egypt audience language, needs, and group signals for household products.

## Inputs

- `brand/audience-profile.md`
- `brand/delta-market.md`
- `evidence/groups-snapshot/beity_177_groups.csv`
- Optional: user-provided comments/screenshots

## Skills to invoke

1. `audience-research`
2. `customer-research`
3. `content-research-and-sourcing`

## Steps

1. Filter groups snapshot by Delta cities / region notes.
2. Extract category language patterns (household, kitchen, جهاز عروسة) as research signals only.
3. Build audience hypotheses labeled as hypotheses.
4. Cite every claim; mark demographics UNKNOWN unless evidenced.

## Guardrails

No private scraping. No fabricated personas with invented stats.

## Outputs for ChatGPT Work

- Audience hypothesis memo
- City coverage map from evidenced group regions
- Quote/language bank only from verified sources
- Research gaps list
