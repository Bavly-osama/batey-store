## Language

- Primary customer language: Egyptian Arabic (عامية مصرية)
- Secondary: Modern Standard Arabic only when formality is required
- English: internal planning / handoff docs; not default customer-facing copy

## Confirmed constraints

- Speak as the brand Page (Beity Store / بيتي ستور / لقطه), not a personal profile.
- Do not invent discounts, stock claims, delivery guarantees, or testimonials.
- Do not use aggressive spam patterns in groups or DMs.

## Working defaults (labeled provisional)

Until E1 and additional Page posts are reviewed:

1. Clear, practical, home-focused language.
2. Benefit-led product description without fabricated social proof.
3. Soft CTA: ask a clarifying question or invite inbox inquiry — never mass-DM automation.
