# Facebook Strategy — Beity Store

## Platform role

Facebook is the primary marketing and social-commerce surface for Beity Store / لقطه.

## Confirmed operating model (evidence-backed)

- Brand publishes / appears as a **Page** identity (Beity Store / بيتي ستور / لقطه), not as a personal profile. Evidence: E2.
- Group distribution is part of the existing business stack (177-group research list). Evidence: E4 snapshot. Publishing automation remains in `X:\bytiey-face-agent` and is out of scope here.
- Official post evidence exists for supervised Page research. Evidence: E1.

## Strategy pillars for Claude Code work

1. **Page content quality** — hooks, captions, product storytelling in Egyptian Arabic (draft only).
2. **Group opportunity research** — evaluate fit via methodology in `group-research-methodology.md`.
3. **Community replies** — draft comment/reply language; never auto-post.
4. **Measurement frameworks** — KPI definitions without invented baselines.

## Out of scope for this workspace

- Auto publish / like / comment / join
- Mass membership requests
- Bulk unsolicited DMs
- Building a second publishing engine
